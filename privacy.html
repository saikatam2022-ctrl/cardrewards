1. Introduction

CardRewards.in is a digital financial services platform operated by [Company legal name — to be filled], a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, with its registered office at [Registered address — to be filled], India.

The platform, accessible via website and mobile interfaces, enables users to explore, compare, and apply for financial products — including credit cards, personal loans, and business financing — offered in partnership with regulated banks and NBFCs.

This Privacy Policy sets out how CardRewards collects, processes, shares, and safeguards personal information obtained through the platform, in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), the Information Technology Act, 2000, and other applicable Indian regulations. Our goal is to be transparent about our data practices and to protect your rights as a data principal.

2. Applicability

This Privacy Policy applies to all individuals who access or use CardRewards's services — whether as applicants, registered users, or referral partners. It covers information you provide directly through the platform, as well as data we may receive from affiliate partners, lending institutions, or other third parties with whom you have consented to share your information.

It also governs data collected automatically through cookies, SDKs, analytics tools, or other tracking technologies integrated into our platform.

3. Data We Collect

CardRewards collects personal information necessary to provide its services. This includes:

  • Identity data: Full name, date of birth, gender, PAN, Aadhaar (where consented), and other government-issued identifiers.
  • Contact data: Mobile number, email address, and city or region.
  • Financial data: Bank account details, IFSC codes, monthly income, credit score (CIBIL), loan amount requirements, and GST number (for business applicants), used primarily for processing applications and commission payouts.
  • Technical data: IP addresses, browser type, device identifiers, geolocation, and session activity, used for security monitoring and platform improvement.
  • Usage data: Browsing behaviour on our platform, product interest, time spent, and interactions with partner links.
  • Communication records: Correspondence with our support team, consent preferences, and opt-in or opt-out choices for marketing communications.

4. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account, verify your identity, and process applications.
  • Match you with appropriate financial products from our lending partners.
  • Track referral activity and calculate and disburse commission payouts accurately.
  • Deliver personalised product offers and relevant marketing communications (where you have consented).
  • Respond to support requests and resolve disputes.
  • Detect fraud, prevent misuse, and maintain platform security.
  • Conduct analytics to improve our services, features, and user experience.
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

In anonymised or aggregated form, your data may also be used for internal research, market reporting, and product development.

5. Legal Basis & User Consent

CardRewards processes personal data on the basis of:

  • Explicit consent — obtained during account registration, form submissions, or when enabling device-level permissions.
  • Contractual necessity — where processing is required to deliver a service you have requested.
  • Legal obligation — where processing is mandated by applicable law, including anti-money laundering regulations or government orders.

You have the right to withdraw your consent at any time by writing to us at [support email — to be filled]. Withdrawal will not affect the lawfulness of prior processing, but may impact the availability of certain services.

6. Sharing & Disclosure of Personal Data

CardRewards shares personal data strictly on a need-to-know basis and only with parties bound by confidentiality and data protection obligations. Data may be shared with:

  • Lending partners (banks and NBFCs) to process credit card or loan applications and confirm eligibility.
  • Affiliate and merchant networks to validate referral transactions and assign cashback or commissions.
  • Third-party service providers engaged for backend operations such as cloud hosting, KYC verification, payment processing, analytics, and email delivery — all of whom are contractually restricted from using the data for unauthorised purposes.
  • Regulatory and legal authorities, where disclosure is required by law or court order, and only to the extent required.

We do not sell your personal data to any third party for commercial purposes.

7. International Data Transfers

In the event that personal data must be transferred outside India — for example, for cloud storage or technical service delivery — such transfers will be conducted in compliance with the provisions of the DPDP Act, 2023. This includes ensuring appropriate data protection measures are in place in the receiving country, or obtaining your explicit consent where required.

8. Data Security

We implement robust, industry-standard security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, disclosure, or misuse:

  • All data in transit is encrypted using SSL/TLS protocols.
  • Data at rest is stored in encrypted formats with access limited to authorised personnel.
  • Regular security assessments and infrastructure audits are conducted.
  • System activity is monitored continuously for anomalies or suspicious behaviour.

In the event of a data breach that is likely to affect your rights, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

9. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain personal data only for as long as is necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including any legal, regulatory, or audit-related requirements. Once data is no longer required, it is either anonymised or securely deleted.

You may request deletion of your account or personal data at any time by contacting our support team. We will honour such requests unless retention is mandated by law or required for ongoing dispute resolution or fraud prevention.

10. Your Rights Under the DPDP Act

As a data principal under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the following rights:

  • The right to access personal data we hold about you.
  • The right to correct inaccurate or outdated information.
  • The right to request erasure of data no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected.
  • The right to nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of incapacity or death.
  • The right to withdraw consent for data processing at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at [support email — to be filled]. We may verify your identity before processing any such request.

11. Age Restrictions

CardRewards is intended exclusively for Indian residents who are legally capable of entering into contracts and are above the age of 18 years. We do not knowingly collect or retain personal data from minors. If we become aware that a minor has submitted personal information, we will delete it promptly upon verification.
